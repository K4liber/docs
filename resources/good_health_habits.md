# Good health habits

## Current Habits

- No alcohol (with the exception of one portion in the sake of "tasting").
- No nicotine.
- No sugar (with the exception of fruits and a one per day dessert).
- Max 3 portions of coffee (1 portion is an espresso or a tea, or a can of soda).
- Finish the last meal by 7pm.
- Target bedtime is 10-11:00pm.
- 8 hours of sleep.
- Half an hour relaxing with memes/music/podcast/book before going to sleep.
- 1-3 portions of electrolytes (magnesium and potassium) every day.
- At least 30 minutes of cardio each morning, before breakfast.
- Calisthenic session 3 times per week (push, pull, core).
- Max naptime is 12 minutes.
- 3 or 4 meals per day - breakfast, lunch, snack/desert (optional), dinner.
- Try to eat less meat and dairy products, and more plant-based foods.

## Old habits

These are habits I used to try, but I no longer practice them anymore:

- Cardio 3 times per week (jogging, cycling, football, etc.).
- No eating dairy products (with the exception of a small amount as one of the negligible component of a meal/food).
- Last meal before 8pm.
- Last meal at least 3 hours before going to sleep.
- Max 3 portions of coffee per day.
- No sugar at all.
- Target bedtime fixed at 10:30pm or 11:00pm.
- Jogging every day.
- 5g of creatine every day.
- Scrolling memes before going to sleep as a way to relax.
- Starting each day with a walk or jogging session + calisthenic session in the afternoon.
